# Lore/Story

No confirmed story/lore for this game, here are a few ideas broken up by category

## What the masks mean

Eg: A mask changes the 'world' you are in but what does that 'world' actually mean in the context of the world building?

**Idea 1: Time periods**

Each mask represents a different time period. Eg: for earth there could be a "future" period. Default state is present. A past in a hotter time period with a thicker rainforests. Apocalyptic time with volcanos everywhere... Could be based on earth or alien planet if we wanted more freedom to make it up

**Idea 2: multiverse**

Same planet but different variants of the same planet. Probably biggest amount of freedom to explore with this one? We could go for a sci-fi theme and call them multiverses or we could go for an ancient civilisation approach and call them "realms" or something beyond our own world.

**Idea 3: invisible forms of matter/energy**

Inspired by the whole dark energy dark matter thing. The alternate masks allow you to interact and see these alternate forms of energy that is interacting with the world. Story-wise this could be linked where you need to use the masks in order to stop the destruction of a world because of something bad happening (could apply to the multiverse one too)
