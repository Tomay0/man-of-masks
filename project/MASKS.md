# Masks

This document describes all the potential ideas for different masks and how they can be implemented. What mechanics and themes they have.

## Default state

It is also important to talk about the theming of what the default state could be and how it is different to the other masks. There is a certain setting to where the player finds the masks initially.

Some ideas:

- Initial setting is in a Forest/Jungle, stumbles on them by 'accident'
- Intial setting is an apocalyptic world, with destroyed stuff and desert - you change masks to areas in the past or other dimensions where this did not happen in order to save it.
- Initial setting is present day earth. Maybe just in a small town or something

## Jungle

Additional features:
- bouncy mushrooms
- vines you can swing off
- trees you can climb

Obstacles:
- Logs blocking paths
- Brambles that can kill you
- insects

## Volcano/Fire

Full of dangerous lava and fireballs. Rock platforms can flow across the lava.

## Ice world

Water becomes 'frozen'. Allows you to stand on water if required, otherwise prevents you going through a waterfall or something similar

Obstacles:
- Falling icicles

## Desert world

Water disappears, areas fill up with quicksand that you can fall in. Sand plumes you can ride up. Cactus or other such plant that kills you

## Spaceship/Future world

Additional features:
- gravity switches, change direction and intensity

Obstacles:
- lasers

Main thematic issue of this one is that it might not 1:1 represent the same location in the world. But I like the idea of being able to switch gravity

## Cloud floaty world

- Lower gravity
- orbs you can bounce off
- bouncy clouds

- lightning as obstacles?
